How ReelScout works

From a product link to 40+ matching videos

ReelScout takes a product name, a product link or a photo, finds at least 20 Instagram Reels and 20 Meta Ad Library video ads that show that product (plus TikTok), scores every video against the product image, and never shows the same video twice.

The pipeline, one search at a time

1

Search request

The dashboard sends a keyword, a product URL or a photo. The API checks the input, blocks unsafe links (internal or private addresses), queues the job and replies at once. Progress then streams live to the browser (Server-Sent Events).

2

Product resolver

For a link, it reads the page in this order: Shopify product feed, structured product data (JSON-LD), Amazon page layout, social preview tags. It extracts the title, description, brand and main image. Results are cached for 3 days.

If the shop blocks bots: a headless browser opens the page instead.

3

Image brain: read the product

Gemini looks at the product photo and returns its type, colours, print, logos, text on the pack, material, shape and 3–5 distinctive details. From these it writes a search plan: exact name, then brand, then descriptive phrases, broad terms and hashtags.

If Gemini is busy: it switches to the next available model; if all are busy, queries are built from the cleaned product title and brand.

5

De-duplication gate

Before anything is downloaded, it drops repeats by platform ID, the same Meta ad running under several IDs, the same video file behind different links, and anything shown in an earlier search. After the thumbnail arrives, it drops re-uploads with a 64-bit image fingerprint (also checked mirrored) and reposts with copied captions.

6

Image brain: score every video

CLIP (running locally, free) compares each thumbnail with the product photo. Gemini then checks the best candidates, 10 thumbnails per call, and gives each a 0–100 score, a one-line reason and a match check for print, colour, shape, logo and visibility.

If a source is still under 20: the search widens to the next queries and repeats, up to 3 rounds, then reports the shortfall in plain words.

7

Dashboard

Tabs per platform with a live count against 20; filters by match, date, text and shortlist; sorting; a detail panel with the score breakdown; search history; and shortlist export to CSV.

How a match score is made

Formula

score = 0.35 × CLIP similarity + 0.65 × Gemini verdict

The vision model decides; CLIP keeps it grounded and picks which videos are worth a Gemini call. For a keyword search with no photo, Gemini judges whether the video shows that type of product.

When Gemini is busy, the score uses visual similarity plus caption evidence; a post by the brand or naming it starts at 60.

Thresholds

70 – 100 Exact match, shown
50 – 69 Close match, shown with a label
0 – 49 Below threshold, hidden unless the filter asks

Every card shows its reason, for example “same skull print and colour, worn by a model”.

Getting TikTok data while TikTok is blocked in India

TikTok's app and website have been blocked in India since 2020, so neither the browser nor the backend can open tiktok.com from here. ReelScout never calls TikTok directly. It asks a data provider whose servers sit outside India, and only talks to that provider's API, which India can reach.

  • The provider returns video ID, caption, creator, date and cover image; ReelScout scores and de-duplicates them like any other source.
  • Playback stays with TikTok: the video itself can't stream in India, so the detail panel shows the cover, caption and score, and offers to copy the link. ReelScout doesn't re-host creators' videos.
  • TikTok sits behind its own toggle and has no minimum, so it can never block Instagram or Meta.

When something goes wrong

Sources

  • Every call has a timeout and up to 2 retries with backoff.
  • A rejected token or used-up credit stops only that source, with a clear message.
  • Raw provider results are cached for 24 hours, so repeats cost no credit.
  • Image-only or carousel ads are dropped; missing thumbnails are marked, never hidden.

Vision model

  • All calls go through one queue at the free-tier limit of 5 per minute.
  • A busy model is skipped for 3 minutes and the next available one is used.
  • If every model is busy, scoring continues on CLIP and captions, then retries Gemini after 2 minutes.
  • Only a rejected API key turns the vision model off.
